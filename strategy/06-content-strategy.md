_Date: 2026-10-10. Sources of truth: `01`–`05`, `docs/LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md`. Tags: [V] verified · [E] estimate · [A] assumption · [H] hypothesis · [U] unknown._

> **Honest starting point.** VIVIA has **no audience, no content history and no user data**. The goal of these 30 days is **learning and recruitment, not reach**. "Validated audience" in the brief does not yet exist: audience and message are still hypotheses (`02`). All Italian copy below is a **draft for native-speaker and clinician review before publication**. Health facts used are limited to sourced statements in `01`; every post carries a source line or a "to verify" flag.

> **Update 2026-10-10 (founder constraints, `10` §1b):** time is **< 10 hours/week** and the budget cap ≈ €2,400. Use a **minimal version of this plan**: **LinkedIn only (2 posts/week), one article, no reels, no ads**, community listening only with moderator permission, and the email-only waitlist. Keep the safeguards in §7. The product referred to is the **Base44 app**; do not publish capability statements until they are re-verified (`11`).

## 1. Objectives and how they are measured

| Objective | Primary measure (not likes or followers) |
|---|---|
| Understand real challenges | Number of **qualified replies** that describe a problem in the person's own words |
| Build trust before asking for downloads or payment | Interview acceptance rate; ratio of questions asked to promises made |
| Practical-expertise credibility | Requests for the checklists; clinician comments |
| Recruit design partners and beta testers | **Waitlist sign-ups from the target segment**; interviews booked |
| Relationships with clinicians, nurses, clinics, associations | Conversations held; partnership next steps agreed |
| Validate which problems drive intent | Engagement *and* sign-up intent by pillar (see §9) |

**Qualified interest** = a person in the target segment (adult living with Crohn's or ulcerative colitis, or a clinician/association contact) who does at least one of: describes a problem unprompted, books an interview, or joins the waitlist. **Activation** (for the 30 days) = completes a first task in the prototype or the pilot (first check-in or first summary), not an app download.

## 3. Language strategy

**Start in Italian.** Reasons: Italy is the initial market (`00`, `10`), the competitive scan found few current Italian IBD tools (`03`, partial evidence), and patient communities and clinicians are most reachable in their own language. **Assumption [A]:** the founder or a close collaborator can write Italian fluently; if not, use a paid native editor. Keep English only for internal documents, international associations and the occasional LinkedIn note.

**Expansion:** add **French** first, then **Spanish and German**, and **Arabic last**, **only when** each condition is met for that language: (1) the Italian pilot shows pull (`10` day-60 gate), (2) a native reviewer and a clinician check the medical wording, (3) a partner or community contact exists in that country. The app already has six interface languages but **only English and Italian were drafted with care; the others are machine-assisted and unreviewed** (`docs/I18N.md`). Arabic additionally needs a real-device right-to-left check.

## 7. Safeguards (apply to every post and comment)

**Never:** diagnose or comment on an individual's symptoms in public; give personal treatment advice; suggest changing, skipping or stopping medication; use fear ("don't miss a flare"); promise outcomes ("fewer flares", "save time"); present AI as a clinician; publish fabricated testimonials or stories; scrape or quote forum posts without permission.

**Required:**
- **Claims log:** each public claim tagged *Verified capability / Hypothesis stated as goal / Roadmap* against `04` §4.
- **Medical facts:** each tied to a cited source, reviewed by a clinician before publication; UK or US data must say so.
- **Moderation reply template (health questions):** "Thank you for sharing. We can't give medical advice here. Please talk to your care team. If you need urgent help, call 112 (in Italy). If you'd like to share your experience with us for research, we'll ask you for consent separately."
- **Patient stories:** written consent for each publication, right to withdraw, option to anonymise; no identifiable health details; no stories from minors.
- **Communities:** ask moderators; read the rules; no promotion where banned; do not record or quote without consent; do not collect health information.
- **Data:** waitlist and interview forms collect name or email and segment only; no health data in marketing forms. Do not paste interview recordings or notes into third-party AI services without an approved privacy basis.

## 9. Reusable templates

**Post template:** Hook (one real situation) → one fact with source or one question → one clear message → one CTA that asks for a conversation, not a download → disclaimer if health-related → claims-log tag.

**Interview invitation (patient):** "Hi [name], I'm [founder]. I'm researching how people with Crohn's or ulcerative colitis prepare for medical visits. Would you give me 30 minutes to talk? I won't ask for medical records or any health information beyond what you want to share; you can stop at any time; I'll keep your name private. If yes, here's a link to choose a time."

**Interview invitation (clinician):** see Day 8 post.

**Community reply (non-medical):** "Thank you for sharing this. We're researching how people prepare for visits, not giving advice. If you'd like to tell us about it, we'd love to hear and will ask for your consent first."

**Consent for a story:** purpose, where it will appear, name shown or anonymous, right to withdraw within X days, contact for removal; signed or confirmed in writing.

**Newsletter template:** one finding · one thing we changed · one question · one invitation · unsubscribe link.

## 11. Weekly review framework

**When:** end of each week (Day 7, 14, 21, 28) plus Day 30, ~1 hour.

| Measure | Definition | Week target (proposed) |
|---|---|---|
| Qualified interest | People meeting the definition in §1 | Cumulative ≥10 by Day 14; ≥25 by Day 30 |
| Interviews completed | Patient + clinician | ≥5 by Day 14; ≥15 by Day 30 |
| Waitlist/beta sign-ups (email only) | Target-segment sign-ups | ≥15 by Day 30 |
| Activation | Completed first prototype task | Reported once a test exists |
| Problem signal by pillar | Replies and sign-up intent per pillar | Rank pillars |
| Trust signal | Questions about privacy; negative comments | Track qualitatively |
| Safety incidents | Medical-advice requests handled by the template; any complaint | **0 unhandled** |

**Decisions each week:** (1) keep, change or drop each pillar; (2) which problem got the strongest unprompted response; (3) any claim that needs correcting; (4) next week's interview targets. Record in `09-feedback-analysis.md`.

**Stop or adjust rules:**
- If after Day 14 there are **fewer than 5 qualified replies**, change the hook or channel before adding posts.
- If a post attracts requests for medical advice repeatedly, add a visible disclaimer and shorten the call to action.
- If the claims log finds an unsupported claim, remove it the same day.
- If the founder-time budget exceeds ≈ 3 h/day, switch to the lean option.
